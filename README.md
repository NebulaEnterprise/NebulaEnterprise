## ⭐ About Nebula

**Nebula**  is an independent, privacy-focused community chat platform built around the idea that communication should remain private.

The name Nebula is inspired by the vast, distant clouds of space, where things disappear into the unknown. It represents our vision for private communication: messages that can be sent, conversations that can be shared, and connections that can be made without leaving a lasting trace.

Just as something sent into the vastness of space eventually disappears from sight, Nebula aims to give users a place where their conversations remain private and their digital footprints are kept to a minimum.

Beyond privacy, Nebula is a place to build communities, exchange ideas, share content, and connect with others — all while putting users' privacy first.

**Your conversations. Your space. Your privacy.**

🔗 **Repository:** https://github.com/NebulaEnterprise/nebula


### 🚀 Features

- 💬 **Direct Messages** — Chat privately with other users.
- 🌐 **Servers** — Create and manage communities.
- 📢 **Channels** — Organize conversations by topic.
- 👥 **Friends** — Connect and communicate with other users.
- 🎙️ **Voice Calls** — Talk with friends and community members.
- 🖥️ **Screen Sharing** — Share your screen with others.
- 👤 **Custom Profiles** — Personalize your identity.
- 📁 **Media Sharing** — Share images, videos, and files.

### 🔒 Privacy & Security

Nebula is built with user privacy in mind.

- Passwords are securely hashed.
- Messages are encrypted on supported client-side workflows.
- Privacy focused data handling.
- No application level IP address logging, according to the project's privacy policy.

### 🛠️ Development

Nebula is an independent software development project.

The website uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while the actual communication features operate through a separate backend.

**Nebula is a chat and community platform.** It is not a financial service, payment processor, advertising network, or malicious software project.

Its purpose is simply to provide people with a place to communicate and build communities.
